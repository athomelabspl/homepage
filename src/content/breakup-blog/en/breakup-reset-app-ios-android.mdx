When you open the app for the first time, you answer a short quiz.

Based on your answers, the app identifies your recovery personality. You might be a Reconnector, Guarded, Storm Rider, or Reset Builder. Every challenge, daily affirmation, and piece of support is filtered through your specific type. This is not generic self-help. The app actually learns how you cope.

## Tools for your hardest days

Breakup Reset helps you get through the aftermath, one small step at a time. Here is what you will find inside.

### 1. Daily challenges and mood analytics
Every day you get three small, achievable challenges matched to your personality. At the start of each session, you log how you are feeling. Over time, the app builds a graph of your emotional history. It even gives you tips based on your own data. You can see exactly which activities resulted in your mood improving over time.

### 2. The No Contact tracker
The no contact rule is a powerful tool to protect your peace. It is also incredibly hard to maintain. Breakup Reset helps you track your streak quietly. If you slip up and text them, you simply reset the clock. There is absolutely no guilt and no shame.

### 3. A strictly private journal
Write it out, exactly your way. The app offers guided prompts and free writing. You can attach a photo to your entry or record a voice note when typing feels like too much work. Most importantly, your journal stays strictly on your device. It is completely private.

### 4. Help me right now
When things get overwhelming, tap the "Help me right now" button on your home screen. You get immediate access to tools designed for panic spikes. You can choose guided breathing exercises, a gentle 100-step walking prompt, or a distraction activity to break your thought loop. Relief is always one tap away.

## Progress you can see

Your milestones in the app grow a visual bamboo plant. Your first journal entry, three days in a row, or two weeks of no contact all become nodes on your plant. There are no flashing points or loud gamification. It is just your story, quietly growing.
