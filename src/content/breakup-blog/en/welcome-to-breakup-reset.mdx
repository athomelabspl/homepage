Breakups are exhausting. Your attention fragments, your habits wobble, and the internet offers a million conflicting opinions. This blog exists to give you one steady place: short, honest articles that respect where you are right now.

## What you'll find here

We'll cover topics like social-media loops, sleep disruption, and the slow work of rebuilding routines. Every article opens with a quick summary so you can decide in seconds whether it's worth your energy.
