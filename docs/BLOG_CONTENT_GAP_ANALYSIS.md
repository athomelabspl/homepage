## Summary

While you have thoroughly covered **internal emotional states** (e.g., anxiety, numbness) and **broad situations** (e.g., long-term relationships, grieving a choice you made), you are missing articles that address:

- **External triggers** (social media, seeing an ex move on)
- **Complex logistics** (shared housing, kids, work)
- **Acute behavioral urges** (urge to text, panic moments)

### Brand alignment check

Breakup Reset is built on the promise of **"small steps, big progress"** and a calm, non-judgmental tone. The new content must continue to:

- Validate these highly specific, messy situations
- Avoid promising a quick fix
- Avoid reading like a clinical textbook

## Recommended content pillars

Highest-value opportunities to pursue next, mapped to search intent and the app's features.

### 1. The "Digital Boundaries & Social Comparison" Cluster

People often suffer secondary heartbreak through social media. You have general "no contact" articles, but modern breakups require digital hygiene strategies.

| Target keyword / keyphrase | Proposed article | Angle | App feature tie-in |
|---|---|---|---|
| should I block my ex / mute vs block | **Muting vs. Blocking: How to Set Digital Boundaries for Your Peace** | Exploring the difference between punishing an ex (blocking in anger) and protecting your own nervous system. | Digital boundary challenges; the non-judgmental No Contact tracker. |
| ex moved on quickly / seeing ex with someone new | **When Your Ex Moves On First (And You Feel Stuck)** | Validating the specific grief spike of social comparison and separating your recovery timeline from theirs. | "Your timeline doesn't depend on theirs" (Bamboo growth timeline); Reframe challenges. |

### 2. The "Complex Logistics" Cluster

Standard breakup advice usually assumes a clean break, but many users are trapped in logistical gray areas where traditional "no contact" is impossible.

| Target keyword / keyphrase | Proposed article | Angle | App feature tie-in |
|---|---|---|---|
| breaking up when living together | **How to Survive a Breakup When You Still Live Together** | Focuses on creating emotional distance inside a shared physical space and the trigger of shared objects. | Environment reset challenges (new bedside setup); the on-device Journal as a strictly private processing space. |
| no contact with kids / work with ex | **How to Do "Low Contact" When You Can't Do "No Contact"** | A realistic guide to functional, boundaried communication for co-parents or coworkers who feel excluded by standard advice. | The Journal (for processing outside of logistics); the Support Hub (for when an email or handover spikes anxiety). |

### 3. The "Betrayal & Identity" Cluster

These are high-volume, emotionally charged searches where Breakup Reset’s gentle, validating tone can really stand out against aggressive or toxic online advice.

| Target keyword / keyphrase | Proposed article | Angle | App feature tie-in |
|---|---|---|---|
| breakup after cheating / betrayal grief | **Why Breakups After Infidelity Are So Complicated** | Untangling the dual grief of losing the relationship and losing trust, while addressing the self-blame loop. | Storm Rider challenges; unsent letters; body scans; grounding techniques. |
| losing friends after breakup / who am I after a breakup | **The Second Loss: Friends, Family, and Identity After a Breakup** | Addressing the social ecosystem that disappears with the relationship and how to slowly rebuild a singular sense of self. | Self-concept clarity; Reconnector social step challenges; "future self" journal prompts. |

### 4. The "Feature-Forward / Acute Action" Cluster

Articles designed to capture users in a moment of panic and convert them by offering immediate, tangible relief.

| Target keyword / keyphrase | Proposed article | Angle | App feature tie-in |
|---|---|---|---|
| how to stop texting ex / urge to text ex | **What to Do in the Next 10 Minutes If You Really Want to Text Your Ex** | A highly actionable, "break glass in case of emergency" guide to riding out the compulsion without shame. | Highlights the "Help me right now" Support Hub (4-1-5 breathing, distraction menu, quick journal). |
| breakup journaling / breakup journal prompts | **Why Your Breakup Journal Shouldn't Live in the Cloud** | Educational piece on the power of unstructured writing, paired with the privacy angle of local-only storage. | The Breakup Reset private, on-device Journal feature. |

## Writing rules (carry forward)

These apply in addition to the standards in the content marketing brief:

1. Lead with the reader's moment, not the feature list.
2. One soft app mention per article max — `<ArticlePromo />` handles conversion.
3. No quiz outcomes as diagnoses.
4. Validate messy logistics without shame (shared housing, kids, work).
5. No quick-fix promises; small steps only.
6. Science hedging always — "research suggests," not "studies prove."
7. Check against published articles before writing to avoid overlap.
